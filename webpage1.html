Institutional Information

Dr RVR and NRI University Vijayawada is a premier educational institution committed to imparting world-class academic standards, fostering research culture, and building professional values in students.

Located in Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh, the university provides state-of-the-art infrastructure, experienced faculty, and strong industry linkages to ensure overall academic excellence and global career opportunities.

Academic Departments

Computer Science & Engineering

Focuses on software engineering, artificial intelligence, data science, cybersecurity, and cutting-edge tech research.

Electronics & Communication

Covers VLSI design, embedded systems, telecommunications, signal processing, and robotics integration.

Mechanical Engineering

Specializes in thermal engineering, industrial design, manufacturing automation, and CAD/CAM technologies.

Business Administration

Offers programs in finance, marketing, human resources, supply chain management, and entrepreneurship.

Admissions Procedure

  1. Online Registration: Fill out the online application form available on the official admissions portal.
  2. Entrance Qualification: Valid entrance exam rank card (EAPCET / PGECET / ICET or University Entrance Exam).
  3. Document Verification: Submit original academic certificates, rank cards, and identity verification documents.
  4. Counseling & Seat Allotment: Participate in counseling sessions for branch selection and final seat confirmation.
